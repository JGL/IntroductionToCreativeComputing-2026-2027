Lecture slides from the 2026-2027 Introduction to Creative Computing unit of Year 0, Block 1 of the Creative Computing Institute's BSc in Creative Computing by Joel Gethin Lewis. 👩🏽‍🎤🤖

Please see GitHub repo for more information and source code for slides.

🔎 Daily Structure:

Tuesday's from 0930 to 1300, from Tuesday 29th September 2026 to Tuesday 8th December 2026, followed by a break for seasonal holidays, resuming on Tuesday 12th January 2027 and finishing on Tuesday TBC.

Daily schedule (all times are London local time):

  • 0930 - 1030: Lecture
  • 1030 - 1045: Break
  • 1045 - 1145: Group work
  • 1145 - 1200: Break
  • 1200 - 1300: Individual work

🗓 Schedule:

  • Tuesday 29th September 2026, Introductions:
    • 👋🏻: Lecture 1: Introduction.
    • 🙋🏻‍♀️: Group work 1: What Git and GitHub are, signing up to GitHub, introductions and connections.
    • 🛠: Individual work 1: Using editor.p5js.org, where to find information about other ways of running p5.js duplicating a p5.js example on editor.p5js.org.
  • Tuesday 6th October 2026, Counting and Remembering:
    • 🔢 & 🐘: Lecture 2: Counting and Remembering.
    • 💭: Group work 2: What's in a name?.
    • 🪞: Individual work 2: Binary <-> Decimal. CamelCase. Converting between types. Code based Data Self Portraits
  • Tuesday 13th October 2026, Functions and Drawing:
    • ⚙️ & ✍🏿: Lecture 3: Functions and Drawing.
    • ☎️: Group work 3: Lillian Schwartz.
    • 🖼: Individual work 3: Drawing Your Initials and Draw, then Code.
  • Tuesday 20th October 2026, Deciding and Looping:
    • 🤔 & 🔁: Lecture 4: Deciding and Looping.
    • ☀️: Group work 4: Sol Lewitt and the Tetralemma.
    • ↔️: Individual work 4: Left or Right, Billiard Ball and Seven Circles.
  • Tuesday 27th October 2026, Arrays and Classes:
    • 📝 & 🚗: Lecture 5: Arrays and Classes.
    • 🧊: Group work 5: Creative uses of multiple dimensions and Timothy Morton.
    • 🖍: Individual work 5: Colour Observation, Living Line I and Ripples in a Pond.
  • Tuesday 3rd November 2026, Randomness and Noise:
    • 🎰 & 📣: Lecture 6: Randomness and Noise.
    • 🪄: Group work 6: Vera Molnar and Alan Moore.
    • 🪙: Individual work 6: Coin toss, Drunk Walk I, II and III.
  • Tuesday 10th November 2026, Scaling and Waves:
    • ⚖️ & 🌊: Lecture 7: Scaling and Waves.
    • 🧶: Group work 7: Anni Albers and Bridget Riley.
    • 🍑: Individual work 7: Butt Generator, Circle using Sine and Cosine.
  • Tuesday 17th November 2026, Time and Algorithms:
    • ⏰ & 🗺: Lecture 8: Time and Algorithms.
    • 📆: Group work 8: Tehching Hsieh, Every Icon, RadicalArt.info.
    • 🌈: Individual work 8: Colour of a Pixel, Searching for the Brightest Point.
  • Tuesday 24th November 2026, Feedback and Emergence:
    • ➰ & 🐣: Lecture 9: Feedback and Emergence.
    • 🌌: Group work 9: Joscha Bach and a Computational Universe.
    • 🦜: Individual work 9: Flocking and Conway's Game of Life.
  • Tuesday 1st December 2026, State Machines and Libraries:
    • 📥 & 📚: Lecture 10: State Machines and Libraries.
    • 🧭: Group work 10: Where next?.
    • 👻: Individual work 10: RiTa.js and p5.gui
